Getting in

No visa

Australian passports get up to 90 days visa-free. Make sure the passport is valid for the whole trip.

No JESTA yet

Japan's e-travel authorisation isn't expected until 2028. Ignore any site charging to "register" you.

Visit Japan Web

Free. Fill it in about a week out to get the QR codes for immigration and customs.

Departure tax

each, already built into the flight price.

Tax-free shopping

You pay full price in store, then claim the 10% back at airport customs before check-in. Keep those purchases in your carry-on.

Skip the JR Pass

For this route, buying train tickets as we go is cheaper than the 14 or 21-day pass.

Cars

Driving at 22

  • OnlyJDM's self-drive tour takes drivers 21 and over who've held a licence for at least 3 years. Most straight rentals want 23+.
  • Bring the original licence, passport and a 1949-convention International Driving Permit from the RAC. No digital copies, no refund if something's missing.
  • Tour is from per car before tax, fuel and tolls included. Car model is assigned on the day.
  • Fallback: a passenger tour with a pro driver from per car. Fri to Sun is 10% more.

Car stops on the route

  • Nissan Crossing, Ginza. Free.
  • Up Garage and A PIT Autobacs, Tokyo. Parts and merch.
  • Ebisu Circuit, Fukushima. Drift hot laps. Closed Wednesdays.
  • Fuji Motorsports Museum, by Fuji Speedway. The bus only runs on weekends, so taxi from Gotemba.
  • Toyota Automobile Museum, Nagoya. , closed Mondays.
  • Mazda Museum, Hiroshima. Free, book ahead.

Nights out, shopping, massage

Bars

  • Golden Gai and Omoide Yokocho in Shinjuku. Expect a small cover charge per bar.
  • Koenji for cheap, local bars.
  • Nomihodai (all-you-can-drink) at izakaya, around for a set time.
  • Sakae in Nagoya and Dotonbori in Osaka on the road.

Shopping

  • Streetwear: Ura-Harajuku, Cat Street, Shimokitazawa, Koenji, Amerikamura.
  • Tech: Yodobashi Akiba, Den Den Town.
  • Retro games: Super Potato in Akihabara.
  • Keep passports handy and do the tax refund at the airport.

Shiatsu

  • Neighbourhood massage chains are far cheaper than hotel or in-room services. Budget an hour.
  • Good after the flight, the Ebisu day and the long train legs.

February weather

  • Cold everywhere, with snow in Takayama and Shirakawa-go. Ebisu will be freezing.
  • Bring warm layers and shoes with grip.
