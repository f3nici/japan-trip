入国 Getting in

Paperwork

No visa

Australian passports get up to 90 days visa-free. Check the passport is valid well past 20 Feb 2027.

No JESTA yet

Japan's e-travel authorisation isn't expected until 2028. Ignore any site charging to "register" you.

Visit Japan Web

Free. Fill it in about a week out for the immigration and customs QR codes. From late 2026 it also handles the tax refund at Narita.

Tax-free changed in Nov 2026

You pay the full tax-inclusive price and claim the 10% back at the airport before bag drop. Scan your passport at a terminal: green means done, red means customs wants to see the goods.

¥5,000 minimum

Per store, per day, before tax. General goods and consumables count together, so it is easier to clear than you might expect. Keep every receipt.

Departure tax

each, already inside the ticket price.

The refund and the shipping don't mix automatically

The refund is confirmed by you exporting the goods, so anything already posted home isn't in your bag when customs asks. If you want the 10% back on a big purchase, either carry it out yourself or ask the store to handle it as a direct export shipment at the counter. Plenty of the big Akihabara and Shinjuku stores do. Worth asking before you pay, not after.

車 Cars

The car half

The one thing still outstanding 国際免許

You've both held licences over three years, so the self-drive convoy is on. What's missing is the International Driving Permit.

  • It must be the 1949 Geneva Convention permit, on paper, from the RAC in WA. Japan does not accept the 1968 version, digital copies or photos.
  • You can only get it in Australia before you leave. There is no way to fix this from Tokyo.
  • Carry the IDP, your actual licence and your passport on the night. Missing any one of them and you don't drive, with no refund.
  • They're quick and cheap, but don't leave it to the last fortnight.

Car stops on this plan 見どころ

  • Daikoku PA, Fri 5 Feb: self-drive convoy. The reason the dates work.
  • Ebisu Circuit, Nihonmatsu, Tue 9 Feb: AE86 Ride & Drive. Closed on major Japanese holidays, which is why it isn't the 11th.
  • Up Garage Machida, the chief store, 35 min on the Odakyu from Shinjuku. Tax-free available.
  • A PIT Autobacs, Shinonome: parts and merch, pairs with Odaiba.
  • Nissan Global HQ Gallery, Yokohama: free, and better than it sounds.
  • Nissan Crossing, Ginza: free, ten minutes, do it on Daikoku morning.
  • Tatsumi PA: late-night car spotting, reachable by taxi.

If the self-drive falls through 代替

The passenger tour with a pro driver runs the same route in the same cars for a car, 10% more Friday to Sunday. No IDP, no paperwork risk on the night, and it puts about back in the shopping money.

Book the self-drive, but know the fallback exists, and that it is a decision you can still make late.

The two nights out 夜遊び

  • Sat 6 Feb: guided izakaya crawl. Someone ordering for you across five or six places is the fastest way to work out what you like.
  • Sat 13 Feb: Golden Gai and Kabukicho on your own. Shoebox bars with six seats and a cover charge, which is the whole point.
  • Omoide Yokocho is the easy default any night, and it's a walk from the hotel.
  • Nomihodai sets run for a fixed time if you want a cheap night.
  • Everything above is within walking distance of a Shinjuku base. That was most of the reason for picking it.

Massage and soaking 整体

  • Four sessions each, spread out: after the flight, after Ebisu, after the first big night, and near the end.
  • Neighbourhood shiatsu chains are an hour and far cheaper than anything the hotel offers.
  • Thermae-yu in Shinjuku is a real onsen with actual hot spring water, walkable from the base.
  • Public sento are about . Tattoos can be an issue at some, Thermae-yu included, so check before you go.

February weather 2月の天気

  • Tokyo runs about 10°C by day and near freezing at night. Dry and often bright, one of the clearest months for seeing Fuji.
  • Ebisu and the Fuji day trips will be considerably colder. Thermals, gloves, a hat.
  • Snow in Tokyo is rare but not impossible, and the city handles it badly when it comes.
  • No snow and no winter sports anywhere in this plan. Fuji is scenery, not an activity.

Why one hotel 拠点

  • Eighteen nights, zero repacking, zero luggage forwarding, zero check-in admin.
  • Shinjuku has the Narita Express direct, plus the JR, Odakyu and Metro lines every day trip on this plan uses.
  • Both big nights out end within walking distance of your bed.
  • Purchases pile up in one room, which makes the shipping day on the 17th far simpler.
  • Book a twin with free cancellation and you can still change your mind.

Getting it all back

With one 23 kg bag each on an Economy Light fare, shipping is the plan rather than a backup. Prices below are from September 2026 and step every 500 g, so a 10.2 kg box bills as 10.5 kg.

Converted from USD at roughly 1.50, so treat them as indicative. Volumetric weight is length × width × height in cm ÷ 5,000. If that beats the scale weight, you pay for the volume, which matters for bulky, light things like figures in boxes.

Australian customs 通関

  • Parcels valued at A$1,000 or less clear with no duty and no GST at the border. That's per consignment, on the whole declared value.
  • Over A$1,000 you need an import declaration, and duty (0–5% on most things) plus 10% GST plus processing fees apply.
  • So split it. Two or three boxes under A$1,000 each beats one over the line, and the extra freight is less than the tax.
  • Declare honestly. Under-declaring a parcel to duck the threshold is not worth it.

What not to put in 検疫

  • Biosecurity blocks meat, dairy, egg, seeds, live plants, untreated wood and anything with soil on it.
  • Sealed commercial snacks and tea are generally fine, so the KitKats will make it.
  • Wooden souvenirs and anything bonsai-adjacent are a problem. Leave them.
  • No lithium batteries or power banks by air freight, and not in your checked bag either.
  • Photograph the contents before you seal a box. It makes an insurance claim possible.
